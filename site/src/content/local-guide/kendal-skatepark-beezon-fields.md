One recent reviewer said...   
  
Pretty chill skatepark, some nice drop ins and the grafitti is actually pretty cool. Not got loads of space but definitely enough for a few people to mess around at the same time. You just have to make sure you go at the right times (during school hours ideally) to avoid being stuck with some quite frustrating/entitled children, aside from them this can be a really nice community when you run into other local skaters.
