As guests staying at Olrig Bank, we hope you’ll make full use of the garden during your stay.

Please feel free to enjoy the garden for games, play and other outdoor activities. We simply ask that, while doing so, you are considerate of the people living nearby and keep noise at a reasonable level.

To that end:

- Game playing and noisy gatherings in the garden must stop after **21:30**.
- Guests who wish to use the garden for a **planned event**, please discuss this with us in advance — this allows us to inform the neighbours that an event is going to take place.

*Thank you for helping us keep things peaceful for our neighbours — we hope you have a wonderful time!*
