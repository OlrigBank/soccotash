# E01-F01 — Booking-first Hero and Compact Quick Check

## Status

Completed on 28 August 2026.

## Parent epic

[E01 — Fine-tuning the Landing Page](./epics/e01-f00-fine-tuning-landing-page.md)

## Objective

Turn the homepage opening into a concise direct-booking step. Visitors should
be able to choose dates, define their party, receive a suitable stay
suggestion and continue without first reading a long proposition or selecting
an arrangement they may not yet understand.

## User experience delivered

### Booking-first hero

The compact booking panel now sits inside the photographic hero in the space
previously occupied by the long introductory copy. The homepage uses the
shorter heading **Stay at Olrig Bank in Kendal** and a concise instruction to
choose dates and define the party.

The former standalone panel below the hero was removed, so the page contains
one primary booking action before **Ways to stay**.

### Compact dates and guests

The initial **Quick Check** presents three compact actions:

1. one combined check-in/check-out control;
2. one guest summary; and
3. **Quick Check**.

Opening the date control reveals the existing accessible calendar. It collects
arrival first and departure second, applies the arrangement's minimum-stay
rule and collapses after a valid range is selected.

Opening the guest summary reveals plus/minus steppers for:

- adults aged 13 and above;
- children aged 2–12;
- infants under 2; and
- pets.

The closed summary reports the current composition, so four number fields no
longer occupy the initial panel.

### Available arrangement

The homepage no longer asks the visitor to choose a stay arrangement before
the check. It ranks enabled standard arrangements by the party size they can
accommodate, then checks availability in ascending capacity order.

The first available match appears only after submission and replaces the
**Quick Check** action. The selected property identifier is then used by the
existing quote API and is carried into the full request URL.

Listing-page panels retain their fixed or explicitly selected arrangements;
automatic suggestion is a homepage option on the shared component.

### Consolidated result

All successful result content is grouped inside the matched-stay card. This
includes, in reading order:

- the total, labelled **total**;
- the matched stay name;
- the **Reserve** continuation action; and
- confirmation that the visitor will not be charged yet.

The host-priced result no longer repeats **Price confirmation required**, the
long price-adjustment explanation and the separate host-review message. An
unavailable result preserves the suggested arrangement and entered inputs
while asking the visitor to choose different dates.

## Implementation notes

- `CompactBookingPanel.astro` gained optional homepage behavior for automatic
  arrangement selection and a configurable submit label.
- The same component retains its established quote, availability, calendar,
  continuation and analytics behavior.
- Date and guest details remain native form values and are submitted through
  the existing request flow.
- The suggestion uses booking property configuration rather than duplicating
  arrangement names or capacities in the homepage.
- The recommendation card contains its own live result region so the price and
  continuation action stay visually and semantically attached to the suggested
  arrangement.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Quick Check is part of the homepage hero and there is no duplicate panel
   immediately below it.
2. The opening state contains a combined dates control, a guest summary and
   **Quick Check**, without a separate Quick Check heading.
3. Adults, children, infants and pets can be changed with plus/minus controls.
4. The homepage does not show an arrangement selector before submission.
5. The smallest suitable available stay is displayed beneath the action
   without a suggested-arrangement label.
6. A match removes the **Quick Check** action and displays, in order, the total,
   stay name, **Reserve** action and no-charge message inside one result card.
7. Continuing preserves arrangement, dates and party composition in `/book/`.
8. Listing-page booking panels retain their prior arrangement behavior.
9. The homepage has no horizontal overflow at the required responsive widths.
