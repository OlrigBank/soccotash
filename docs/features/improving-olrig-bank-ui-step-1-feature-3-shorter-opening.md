# Feature 3 — Shorter Homepage Opening

## Parent epic

[Improving the Olrig Bank UI — Step 1](./epics/improving-olrig-bank-ui-1.md)

## Objective

Move visitors directly from the new hero to the four Ways to stay without a
second large introductory panel.

## Scope

- Remove the standalone homepage prose panel.
- Add a stable `ways-to-stay` fragment target.
- Keep no more than two short orientation sentences with the section heading.
- Retain a concise route to Jenna for help choosing an option.
- Preserve the agreed card order and existing listing links.

## Acceptance criteria

1. Ways to stay follows the hero directly.
2. The four options remain Olrig Bank, The Cottage at Olrig Bank, Olrig Bank Max
   and Olrig Bank Bespoke, in that order.
3. All detailed accommodation links remain crawlable without JavaScript.
4. The heading appears within or close to the second screen at 390 × 844.
