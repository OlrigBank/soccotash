# E01-F02 — Homepage Discovery and Direct-booking Finish

## Status

Completed on 28 August 2026.

## Parent epic

[E01 — Fine-tuning the Landing Page](./epics/e01-f00-fine-tuning-landing-page.md)

## Objective

Reshape the content after the booking-first hero so it helps visitors picture
the property, understand the available stay arrangements, discover curated
local recommendations and reach a clear direct-booking action without the
former grids of repetitive cards.

## User experience delivered

### Olrig Bank photo gallery

A six-image **Olrig Bank in pictures** gallery now follows the homepage hero.
Its horizontal, scroll-snapping rail works at touch widths and expands from
one dominant image on mobile to multiple visible images on wider screens.

Visitors can open any photograph in a full-screen dialog. The viewer supports
previous and next controls, left and right arrow keys, backdrop dismissal and
a dedicated close button. Each thumbnail and full-size image has descriptive
alternative text and a visible caption.

### Ways to stay at Olrig Bank

The section is now headed **Ways to stay at Olrig Bank** and places the
established Olrig Bank exterior image before the introductory text. The text
describes the property's history and summarises the three standard packages,
with links to each listing, plus routes to a bespoke request and help from
Jenna.

The former four-card accommodation grid was removed. This keeps the section
compact and avoids repeating choices already made by Quick Check while
preserving direct routes to every stay option.

### Curated local guide panel

The former six-card **Featured local guide** grid was replaced by one focused
panel using the Kendal Castle image. The **Plan your stay** introduction
explains the value of Olrig Bank's curated recommendations and links to the
complete local guide.

The panel is stacked on mobile and becomes a balanced image-and-copy layout on
wider screens.

### Direct-booking page finish

The site footer now ends public pages with a dark **Book direct** section,
concise benefit-led copy, a `/book/` action and links to the site's main public
destinations. A single sticky **Check availability** action remains visible at
mobile widths. Non-working WhatsApp actions considered during review were
removed before completion.

## Implementation notes

- `HomeGallery.astro` owns the gallery data, accessible controls, dialog and
  responsive presentation.
- The homepage uses existing repository assets for the Olrig Bank exterior and
  Kendal Castle rather than introducing new image files.
- The local guide promotion is deliberately stable and does not depend on the
  database's current recommended-entry ordering.
- Footer navigation and booking actions use existing public routes.
- The mobile action bar includes safe-area padding and the page reserves space
  for it so content is not obscured.

## Acceptance criteria

1. The image gallery appears after the booking-first hero and works with touch,
   mouse and keyboard interaction.
2. **Ways to stay at Olrig Bank** includes the Olrig Bank image before its text
   and retains links to all standard and bespoke choices.
3. The homepage no longer renders accommodation or featured-guide card grids.
4. One Kendal Castle panel promotes the curated local guide under **Plan your
   stay**.
5. Public pages finish with a direct-booking footer and valid navigation.
6. Mobile visitors have one working sticky **Check availability** action and no
   WhatsApp button.
7. The responsive layout has no horizontal overflow at the review widths.
